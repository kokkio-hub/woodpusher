You only need to give a username and a password if you want an account, for online games and to keep your progress across devices. Without an account you can still play Stockfish, solve puzzles, practise openings and review games.

Woodpusher asks for no email address, shows no ads, and uses no tracking or analytics. Fonts are served from this site itself, not from Google.

Your settings, and the last username you looked up under Review a game, are saved only in your own browser (local storage). Without an account, your puzzle progress, opening progress and results against Stockfish (used for the estimated Elo in Settings) stay in your browser too. Once you log in they are also saved to your account, as in the table above. When you log out, the browser forgets them; logging in again brings them back.

The ready-made messages in online games (“Good luck”, “Good game” and so on) are kept only in the server’s memory for the game and its first ten minutes afterwards, and are never written down.

When you look up games by username, Woodpusher's server asks Lichess or Chess.com for that player's public games. Those sites see a request from Woodpusher's server, not from you, and the answer is kept in the server's memory for two minutes. If you install Woodpusher as an app, your browser keeps a copy of the site's files so it also opens without a connection; that copy holds no personal data. The same goes for the full chess engine that a deep review downloads (99 MB): your browser keeps it for later deep reviews, and you can remove it under Settings → Deep review.

What others can see

Your profile is public: anyone, also without an account, can see your username, the date you joined, your ratings and rating history, your wins, draws and losses, and your recent games. Once you have played a rated game in a time control, you can appear in the top 50 of the leaderboard for it. If your account is closed for fair play, your profile says so. Who reported you is never shown to you or to anyone else; only the admin sees it.

Who processes the data

The site and database run on Railway (Railway Corporation, San Francisco, USA), which processes the data on my behalf. Woodpusher's servers are in the United States (Virginia). Because that is outside the EU, the transfer is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Railway's data processing agreement.

The domain is managed by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., San Francisco, USA). When you open woodpusher.org without “www”, your request first goes through Cloudflare, which sends you on to www.woodpusher.org. For that request Cloudflare sees your IP address and the details your browser sends, such as its type and language. If you start a deep review, your browser downloads the full Stockfish engine from engine.woodpusher.org, which is stored at Cloudflare (R2), and Cloudflare sees the same details for that download; just opening a review does not contact it. Cloudflare does all this on my behalf under its data processing agreement, which covers the transfer to the United States through the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as a fallback.

Emails you send me arrive in my Gmail mailbox, which Google (Google Ireland Limited) provides.

Apart from these, and the lookups at Lichess and Chess.com described above, nobody receives your data.

Your rights

You can see your data on your profile and in your game history, and copy any game as PGN. You can delete your account at any time under Play online → Delete account. This removes your account, ratings, saved progress, fair-play results and reports about you, and takes your name out of past games. If your account was closed and you think that was a mistake, email the address above. For anything else (access, correction, restriction, objection or a copy of your data), email oscarkok35@gmail.com. I will answer within a month.

You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Age

Woodpusher accounts are meant for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

When this statement changes, the date at the top changes with it. I will also mention important changes on the site itself, such as a new party that receives your data.

Open source

The chess engine is Stockfish.js (GPL-3.0). Woodpusher's own source code is at github.com/kokkio-hub/woodpusher.
